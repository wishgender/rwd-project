About Hilltop

Who We Are

Hilltop Productions is Kent State University Student Media's video production studio. We have previously been known as TV2's production department, then as Kent State Student Media Production and Film Distribution, but we rebranded in 2026, officially becoming Hilltop Productions. As a media partner of Kent State Student Media, Hilltop Productions is wholly student-run, including the entirety of our staff.
