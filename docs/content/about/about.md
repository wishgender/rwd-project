# About Us

## Who We Are

Hilltop Productions is Kent State University's very own student-run video production company. Originally Kent State Student Media Production & Film Distribution, Hilltop provides tech crews for the production of KentStaterTV News, KSTV Sports, and Black Squirrel Entertainment. We also maintain a [livestream](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/kent) of student-produced content that runs 24/7/365. Outside of the studio, our On-Demand Unit (ODU) offers video production services for on-campus organizations and local businesses.
